## A return path

To keep something is to leave a path back to it. The path can be a filename, a drawer, a precise sentence, or a familiar place on a desk.

The object matters less than the possibility of a return that does not begin from zero.

## Enough structure

The best private systems are light enough to be maintained on an exhausted day. Anything more elaborate becomes another object waiting to be kept.
